Symbols

One table for every symbol the lessons use. The standard column is what new pages use; the "also written" column lists older spellings that still appear in some units.

StandardMeaningAlso written
Z = (X, A, Y)One observation: covariates X, treatment A ∈ {0, 1}, outcome Y. A lower-case z is a possible value of Z; z₀ is one specific patient’s value.O, (W, A, Y)
PThe true distribution of Z. A point in the space of distributions; the model 𝓜 is the set of points allowed.P₀
PₙThe empirical distribution: mass 1/n at each observed Zᵢ. Also a point in the same space. Pₙf means the sample average of f.
P̂The initial fit (the plug-in’s distribution), usually from a machine-learned outcome model and propensity.Q̂, P̂ₙ
ψ(P)The target parameter, a map from distributions to numbers. For this chapter, E[μ₁(X)] or the ATE E[μ₁(X) − μ₀(X)].Ψ(P), ψ₀
μₐ(x) = E[Y | A = a, X = x]The outcome regression in arm a. μ̂ₐ is its fit.Q̄(a, x), Q̂, mₐ(x)
π(x) = P(A = 1 | X = x)The propensity score. π̂ is its fit.g(x), e(x)
D(z) or D_P(z)The efficient influence function of ψ at P: the one function with E_P[D·h] = dψ/dε for every score h, mean zero under P. D(P̂) is it evaluated at the fit.ϕ(z), ϕ̂, D*(P)(O), EIF
H(a, x) = a/π(x) − (1−a)/(1−π(x))The clever covariate: the coefficient of the residual Y − μₐ(X) in D. For E[Y(1)] alone it is a/π(x).Hₙ, H*(A, W)
p_ε = p(1 + εh)A path (one-parameter family) through P in direction h. The linear tilt; the exponential tilt ∝ p·exp(εh) is used where large steps are needed.P_ε, {P_ε}
h(z)A score: d/dε log p_ε(z) at ε = 0. Mean zero under P because total mass stays one.S(z), score
εThe step size along a path. In TMLE, the coefficient in μ_ε = μ̂ + εH, fit by maximum likelihood; ε̂ solves PₙD(P̂_ε̂) = 0.
ψ(P̂) + PₙD(P̂)The one-step estimator: plug-in plus the sample mean of the influence function at the fit. Equals AIPW for the ATE.ψ̂⁺, ψ̂_OS
R₂(P̂, P)The second-order remainder in ψ(P̂) − ψ(P) = −P D(P̂) + R₂. For the ATE, a product of the propensity and outcome-model errors.R(P̂, P), Rem
(Pₙ − P)(D(P̂) − D(P))The empirical-process term: a sample average of a function learned from the same sample. Cross-fitting makes it small.
𝒯, T(P)The tangent space: the closure of all scores at P. In a nonparametric model, every mean-zero finite-variance function.

Units 2, 3, 4 and 7 were written with ϕ for the influence function; newer units use D. They are the same object.
