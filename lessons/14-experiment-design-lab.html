What should we randomize?

Two zones, one shared fleet. Step through six requests by hand, then compare request-level randomization with randomized switchbacks against the full-policy reference, and see what a switch does not reset.

Further reading: Bojinov, Simchi-Levi and Zhao: Design and analysis of switchback experiments. Every number on this page is produced by the accompanying fleet simulator (science/marketplace.js), whose rules are stated at the top of that file and checked against a hand-worked fixture; the reference is a Monte Carlo quantity with its own uncertainty, not an exact truth.
