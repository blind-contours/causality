Targeted survival curves and ΔRMST

Weight for censoring, augment with the efficient influence function, and target survival at a horizon and the RMST difference, with an interval you can defend.

What is exact, simulated and schematic: the true curves, RMSTs and the efficiency bound E[D²] are computed exactly from the data-generating law (discrete months, three severity levels). The single study on each step is one seeded simulation of 1000 patients, analysed live with pooled logistic hazard models, so its numbers change when you draw a new study. The double-robustness histograms are 1000 precomputed seeded studies of 800 patients each (scripts/targeted-survival-precompute.cjs). The derivation is exact for this discrete-time model; the risk-set strip shows 16 patients per severity level as a picture of the whole treated arm, whose counts are in the table. The moving-mass figure uses the course cohort's 58 treated patients with a severity-dependent dropout rate; its curves and weights are exact for those patients, and its truth is their own simulated event times.
