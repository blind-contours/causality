Compute it yourself

Four Patients

Every number in the earlier units came out of a simulation you watched. Here there are four patients, and you do the arithmetic. Type each value, check it, and see the mean of your four numbers equal the one-step correction. Then fit ε by hand for four residuals. If you can do this page from a blank sheet, you understand the estimator.

Part 1

The influence function of E[Y(1)], one patient at a time

The target is ψ = E[μ1(X)], the mean outcome if everyone were treated. The initial fit gives μ̂1(x) for each patient and a propensity π(x). The influence function at the fit has two pieces: the X-piece μ̂1(Xi) − ψ̂, which is filled in for you and sums to zero, and the Y-piece Hi·(Yi − μ̂1(Xi)) with Hi = Ai/π(Xi), which you compute. Untreated patients have H = 0: they say nothing about μ1. Then average the Y-pieces. That average is the one-step correction, and ψ̂ plus it is the one-step estimate.

D̂(Zᵢ) = [μ̂₁(Xᵢ) − ψ̂] + Hᵢ (Yᵢ − μ̂₁(Xᵢ)), Hᵢ = Aᵢ / π(Xᵢ), ψ̂ = mean of μ̂₁(Xᵢ)
one-step = ψ̂ + (1/n) Σᵢ Hᵢ (Yᵢ − μ̂₁(Xᵢ))
PatientStratumAYπ(X)μ̂₁(X)X-pieceH = A/πY − μ̂₁H·(Y − μ̂₁)
Fill the three right-hand columns for each patient, then the sums and the mean at the bottom.
In one sentenceEach treated patient contributes its residual scaled by 1/π, each untreated patient contributes nothing to the outcome piece, and the average of those contributions is exactly the correction the one-step adds to the plug-in.
Part 2

ε̂ by hand: four treated residuals, two strata

Now the TMLE step. Four treated patients, two young with H = 1.25 and two old with H = 4. Fluctuate με = μ̂ + εH and pick ε by least squares: ε̂ = ΣHiri / ΣHi². Compute the two sums, divide, and then the shift each stratum's curve gets, ε̂·H. Notice which stratum's residuals dominate the numerator even though there are the same number of them.

ε̂ = Σ Hᵢ rᵢ / Σ Hᵢ² shift of stratum s = ε̂ · Hₛ check: Σ Hᵢ (rᵢ − ε̂ Hᵢ) = 0
PatientStratumHr = Y − μ̂H·rH²
ε̂ = ΣHr / ΣH²young shift ε̂·1.25old shift ε̂·4
In one sentenceε̂ is the no-intercept least-squares slope of the residuals on the clever covariate. The old stratum’s two residuals enter the numerator with weight 4 against 1.25, which is the reweighting that gives every stratum evidence in proportion to its share of ψ.
