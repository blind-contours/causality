Does the evidence support the decision?

Repeated experiments on a shared fleet, a validated switchback benchmark, calibration against a named target, and a one-page recommendation.

Further reading: Bojinov, Simchi-Levi and Zhao: Design and analysis of switchback experiments. The fleet simulator and the finite-history benchmark live in science/marketplace.js; the larger Python grid, when present, is regenerated by the command printed beside the comparison panel. Simulation results are evidence for the simulated settings, not a proof of general validity.
